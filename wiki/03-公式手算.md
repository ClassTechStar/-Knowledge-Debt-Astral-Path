# BASELINE 公式手算例（Wiki-03）

## score

```text
score = 100 × (0.6×recent_acc + 0.3×sev_norm + 0.1×(self_conf/5))
sev_norm = 1 − min(sev, 1)
```

| 金样 | recent_acc | sev | self_conf | 手算 | expect |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|
| G-SCORE-1 | 0.30 | 0.80 | 2 | 100*(0.6*0.30+0.3*0.20+0.1*(2/5)) | 28.0 |
| G-SCORE-2 | 0.40 | 0.60 | 4 | 100*(0.6*0.40+0.3*0.40+0.1*(4/5)) | 44.0 |
| G-SCORE-3 | 0.80 | 0.30 | 4 | 100*(0.6*0.80+0.3*0.70+0.1*(4/5)) | 77.0 |

## impact

```text
recency = 1/(1+days/7)
impact = freq × (50 − score_c) × recency × weight
触发：score_p<40 ∧ score_c<50 ∧ freq>0
```

演示主债边：

```text
score_p=28, score_c=41, freq=6, days=0, weight=1.6
recency=1
impact=6×(50−41)×1×1.6=86.4
```
